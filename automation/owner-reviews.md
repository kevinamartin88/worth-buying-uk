# Optional owner-review enrichment

Daily publishing remains independent of review research. The renderer reads a
small curated evidence file and never calls a retailer or social network during
publication. Missing, malformed, expired or unmatched evidence adds no section;
it must not prevent the original guide from publishing.

Research may use retailer product reviews, manufacturer reviews and public
social discussions. If owner comments are unavailable, research reputable
independent review sites and the exact model's manufacturer details instead.
Use source_kind customer_reviews, social_discussion, editorial_review or
manufacturer_details so published attribution fits the actual evidence. Never
describe a manufacturer claim as owner feedback or an independent test result.
No empty-state message or research failure is shown to readers. The renderer
does not discover new sources; source research and curation happen separately
from daily publication so network outages cannot block an article.
Social authors' ownership and verification must be stated
accurately. Match the exact model, capacity and relevant variant; do not reuse
comments from related products or conflate seller feedback with product reviews.
Read the source and record its URL, review date, expiry, meaningful praise,
drawbacks or limits and a balanced buying judgement. Do not invent a complaint
to create balance. If there is no reliable negative evidence, explain the sample
limits and practical checks. Use brief paraphrases rather than copied reviews.
Exclude delivery-only comments and comments explicitly made before product use
from performance evidence; mention these as limits when necessary.

Never imply hands-on testing, verified ownership or consensus without evidence.
Do not generate Review or aggregateRating schema from other sites' ratings.
Do not copy large threads or identify social users unnecessarily. Source pages
can group variants or syndicated reviews; inspect the model scope carefully.

The initial evidence covers Tower T16043GRY (UK, B&Q) and the Crock-Pot Design
Series 7-Quart with Thermal Bag (USA, eBay). No social evidence is seeded until
a matching public discussion has been read and verified. Future daily picks
receive the section only when their model matches an unexpired evidence entry.
