- Added `src/us_targeting.py`: case-aware en-US wording, explicit US/English metadata, USD and UK-copy rejection, URL validation, and approximate US-unit equivalents retaining the original manufacturer measurements. ZIP codes, sales tax, US shipping, warranty/returns and electrical compatibility appear in USA article context.
- Cleaned all 35 stored USA articles, including titles, body text, labels, search descriptions, social text and video points. Stable slugs and existing Blogger paths remain intact. Price values and retailer targets are not converted or guessed. Concurrent retailer-price/article updates were retained.
- Applied the USA policy to daily, supporting, weekly savvy-buyer, market-watch, Rakuten, bespoke and Prime article builders, and to direct Blogger/manual-package publishing, authority pages and discount pages. Reconciliation localizes existing manual post copy when needed while retaining its body and images.

Current approved hosts: amazon.com, ebay.com, iherb.com, sharperimage.com, walmart.com, target.com, bestbuy.com, homedepot.com, lowes.com and costco.com (with optional www). Rakuten links require an inspectable `murl` destination on that list. USA Rakuten price records without explicit USD are discarded. New merchants require review and an allowlist update. Existing eBay domestic-stock and USD checks, Amazon.com exact-offer checks and USA affiliate tags are retained.
- Google Shopping and Trends already requested US geography. USA topic names/search terms are now localized before ranking; USA research user agents reference the USA site; cached USA Shopping signals without `geo=US` are rejected. Tests inspect US/en-US/froogle request parameters and the Trends RSS geography. No UK or site-traffic signal is substituted for US Shopping demand.
- USA content plan now uses `https://www.worthbuyingusa.com/`, America/New_York, en-US, USD and explicit US research geography. The central domain config already had the correct public origin. Blogspot is retained only as a recognized Blogger backend alias.
- USA X, Bluesky, Pinterest and Shorts normalize known USA aliases to HTTPS/www while preserving article paths and reject cross-site destinations. X custom post text may link only to the USA site. USA Buffer selection now requires a unique matching USA channel and cannot fall back to a sole UK account. This does not edit external account bios or old social posts.
- USA Blogger publishing now retries at **14:23, 17:23 and 21:23 UTC** daily: **10:23/13:23/17:23 Eastern during daylight time**, **09:23/12:23/16:23 Eastern during standard time**. Scheduled, push and manual publishing attempts outside 09:00–21:00 Eastern defer to the next scheduled run. X, Bluesky, Shorts and bespoke USA X promotions use the same daylight-saving-aware window. UK overnight generation and UK publishing schedules remain unchanged; USA drafts can still be generated overnight before daytime publication. Pinterest's existing 21:00 UTC sync already fits US audience hours and was retained. Discount/authority maintenance retains its existing schedule.
- Added a Blogger-compatible self-referencing en-US head snippet at `automation/blogger-us-head.xml`. It keeps Blogger's canonical machinery and only emits on the configured USA host. Post-publish discovery checks now report absent or incorrect en-US hreflang. No head tags were inserted into article bodies and no unverified UK alternate was claimed.
- Replaced the large-capacity USA air-fryer hero's oven artwork with countertop basket air fryers. Versioned its Blogger/X image URL to avoid reusing cached artwork, and made future USA air-fryer generation explicitly request basket handles/drawers and exclude ovens. Product listing photographs remain their original retailer images, preserving their association with the listed model. The generated hero is editorial illustration, not a photo of a particular offer.
- Added `tests/test_us_targeting.py` to the content-quality and USA publishing checks. Updated the USA Rakuten test fixture to include its inspectable merchant destination.
